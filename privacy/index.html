Moonlight — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Moonlight is a shift and income tracker for clinicians. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, what leaves your device, and what never does.

The short version: Moonlight works entirely offline and keeps your data on your iPhone. An account is optional. If you sign in, most of your data is copied to your private account so it can be restored on another phone; if you don't, nothing is sent to us at all. We do not use advertising or tracking of any kind, and the app doesn't report how you use it. Apple's privacy label lists purchase history and analytics only because the app contains RevenueCat's in-app purchase software, which this version never turns on (section 9).

1. Who we are

Moonlight is published by October Fourteen LLC, which is the controller of the data described below.

Contact: support@moonlightshifts.com

2. What Moonlight stores on your iPhone

Everything you enter is written to a private database inside the app's own storage area, which other apps cannot read:

  • Shifts — dates, times, breaks, pay structures, calculated and actual amounts, and any free-text notes you add.
  • Workplaces — employer name, pay rates, schedule and paydays, and any contact name, email, phone or address you choose to record.
  • Shift types, scheduled shifts, income goals, expenses and mileage, paycheck reconciliation records, and timer sessions.
  • Credentials and CME — licence, DEA and certification records, their expiry dates and reference numbers, plus CME entries.
  • Connected schedules — the link to an Amion or other iCal feed, if you connect one.
  • App preferences — currency, week start, theme, notification and reminder settings.

3. What leaves your device if you sign in

Signing in is optional, and until you do, nothing is sent to us. If you create an account, the following is copied to our cloud database so it can be restored on another device, and kept up to date while you stay signed in:

  • Shifts — dates, times, breaks, amounts, override reasons, paid amounts and dates, and your notes.
  • Workplaces — name, pay details, schedule and paydays, and any contact name, email, phone or address you entered for that workplace.
  • Shift types — names, colours and pay structures.
  • Scheduled shifts — planned times, notes, and the shift name and event identifier from a connected feed (never the feed's link).
  • Expenses — category, amount, miles, date, note, and the shift it's for, if any.
  • Income goals, paycheck reconciliation records and timer sessions.
  • Preferences — currency, week start, theme, notification and reminder settings, quiet hours, and saved colours.

What is never uploaded, even when signed in

These stay on your device. There is no place on our server to put them at all:

  • Credentials and CME records — including DEA numbers, medical licence numbers, certification details and their notes.
  • Connected schedule links — the feed address often contains a private token that grants access to your schedule, so it is never uploaded to your account.
  • Your Apple Calendar connection — which calendar you mirror shifts into, and whether amounts are included.

Because these are never uploaded, signing in on a new phone does not bring them back. Only an iPhone backup does (section 8).

⚠️ About notes — please read

Notes on shifts, expenses and scheduled shifts are free text, and they sync to your account when you are signed in. They are also included in CSV exports.

Do not put patient-identifiable information in them. Moonlight is a personal income tracker, not a clinical record system, and it is not designed, secured, or contracted to hold protected health information. Use notes for things like "held over for handover" or "promised a differential" — not patient names, medical record numbers, or clinical details.

4. Your account

If you sign in, we store your email address for the sole purpose of identifying your account and sending you a one-time sign-in code. We never send marketing email.

Moonlight uses passwordless email sign-in. There is no password to store, and we do not offer or use any third-party or social login, so no social-login provider learns from it that you use this app.

5. Who processes your data

We use Supabase (https://supabase.com) to host the database and handle sign-in. Your account data is stored in the United States. Supabase acts as our processor and does not use your data for its own purposes.

Every row in the database is protected by row-level security: the database itself enforces that only your own account can read or write your data, not just the app.

6. The other connections the app makes

Two features move data off the phone once you turn them on, signed in or not. Neither sends anything to us.

  • Connecting a schedule feed. If you add an Amion, QGenda or other iCal link, Moonlight fetches that address directly from your device to read your schedule. The request goes to whoever hosts that feed, not to us. It carries none of your Moonlight data, but like any web request it tells that host your device's IP address and when you asked. If you set the feed to refresh daily or every six hours, this happens on its own each time you open the app, not only when you tap Re-sync. Set the feed to manual if you would rather it only ever happen when you ask.
  • Mirroring shifts to Apple Calendar. If you turn this on, each scheduled shift is written into a calendar you choose, using Apple's own on-device calendar. The event's title is your shift type and employer name, and the expected amount is included only if you switch that on separately. What happens to that calendar afterwards — iCloud sync, a work account your employer can see — is governed by your iOS settings and Apple's policies, not by Moonlight.

7. Files and messages you send yourself

Settings → Export data produces six things: a CSV of your shifts, a CSV of your expenses, a period earnings report PDF, a 1099 income summary PDF, a deductions summary PDF, and a pay reconciliation PDF. The shift CSV includes your shift notes and the expense CSV includes your expense notes; the PDFs carry employer names, shift dates and types, expense categories and amounts but no notes. None of the six includes anything from Credentials or CME.

Separately, when a paycheck comes up short, Draft a note composes a short message — your employer's name, the shift, and the two amounts — and hands it to the share sheet. Nothing is addressed or sent for you.

In every case the file or message goes to whatever you pick from the iOS share sheet — Mail, Messages, Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop — and once it leaves the app it is outside Moonlight's control.

8. Backups

An encrypted iCloud or Finder backup of your iPhone includes Moonlight's database, credentials and CME records included, and restores it along with the app. That backup is Apple's, governed by your iCloud settings; we have no access to it.

9. What we do not do

  • No usage reporting. The app has no SDK that reports on how you use it: no telemetry and no crash reporting. It does contain RevenueCat's in-app purchase software (https://www.revenuecat.com), but this version sells nothing and never turns it on, so it sends RevenueCat nothing. Apple's privacy label still lists purchase history, and analytics as a use of it and of your user ID, because that software is in the app.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no ad networks.
  • No tracking. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not share, sell, or broker your data with anyone.
  • No crash reporting service, no profiling, and no automated decision-making.

10. Deleting your data

  • Delete your account — in the app, under Settings → Account & sync. This permanently deletes your account and everything synced to it, and removes Moonlight's data from that phone, including the credential and CME records that were never uploaded. It cannot be undone. Another phone that still has your account's data keeps it until you remove the data there too (below).
  • Remove data from one phone — Settings → Account & sync → Sign out & remove data. This clears everything stored on that phone, including credential and CME records, and leaves your account as it is. On a phone already signed out that still holds an account's data, the same screen offers Remove data from this phone.
  • Both, and switching the phone to another account, also delete the shift events that phone added to your Apple Calendar, if Moonlight still has access to the calendar.
  • Ordinary sign-out leaves your data on the phone so you can sign back in.
  • Deleting a single record — deleting a shift, scheduled shift, expense, goal, paycheck flag, credential or CME record erases the text you typed on it (its notes, a goal's label, a CME's title, a credential's numbers and issuer) from the phone, and from your account if it had synced there. The record itself stays, marked deleted, with its dates and amounts, so your other devices remove it too. A deleted credential keeps its name, and a deleted shift type or employer keeps its name because the shifts that used it still show it; an employer's contact details are erased.
  • Not signed in — delete the app to remove its database and everything in it. If an iPhone backup includes Moonlight, delete or replace that backup too.

When you delete your account, your synced data is removed immediately by the database itself; we keep no separate copy. Our hosting provider's routine infrastructure backups, if any, rotate out within 7 days. We keep your data for as long as your account exists and do not retain it after deletion.

11. Security

  • Data in transit is encrypted with TLS.
  • Your sign-in session is stored encrypted on the phone, with a key held in the iOS Keychain that cannot be restored onto a different device.
  • Access to your rows is enforced by the database, per account.
  • On the phone, your data sits in the app's own storage area, which iOS protects with the device passcode and hardware encryption. Keep a passcode set.

No system is perfectly secure, but we do not store anything we do not need.

12. Children

Moonlight is intended for working clinicians and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data. Moonlight gives you all of these directly in the app: your data is visible and editable throughout, exportable from Settings → Export data, and deletable as described above. For anything else, contact us at support@moonlightshifts.com.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects or where it goes, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will be surfaced in the app before they apply to you.

15. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@moonlightshifts.com
